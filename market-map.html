How this map works ✕
Branches & Zones
Circle size is deposits — a bigger circle means more deposits at that branch. Color is Opportunity Zone, the same scoring used across every Verlocity tool:
Invest — top opportunity, put resources here now Analyze — real opportunity, worth validating first Defend — share worth protecting, don't over-invest Justify — rebalance resources toward stronger branches
Priority Branches
A different lens on the same branches — which ones have a specific recommended action ("play") assigned right now:
Top Priority — urgent, act now Next — strong position, extend it Opportunity — real potential, worth testing Everything else — scored, just not flagged urgent
Competitors
Every competitor branch within 10 miles of any of your branches — shown as diamonds so they're never confused with your own circles. Colored by their own Opportunity Zone, since the same scoring runs network-wide, not just for you.
Persona / Zip
Who lives in a zip code — dominant occupation, income, age, estimated households. Click any shaded area for the detail card.
Top Cities
Splits your best cities into two separate questions, because they're not the same thing:
Priority — you already have strength here, and nearby competitors show signs of weakness. Good for acting now. Opportunity — a real, earlier-stage play worth testing.
⚠️ This ranks near-term capture potential — play type × your branch size × how weak nearby competitors currently are. It does not yet weigh population, income, or housing growth. A market can still be a strong long-term bet even if it doesn't appear here, if local competitors happen to be strong too.
Click a branch → Radius Analysis
Click any branch to draw 1/3/10-mile rings and see every competitor in range, your market share at each radius, and a full competitor table with deposits and rate trends.
Generate Market Story
Turns everything currently on screen into a boardroom-ready narrative, written live when you click it — not a pre-written template.
Data freshness
Deposit figures follow FDIC's annual release; demographic figures follow the Census Bureau's annual release. This is not real-time or daily data.
