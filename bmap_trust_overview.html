Data & Access — BMAP / Verlocity Growth System
What we touch, where it lives,
and what has to happen before it changes.
The direct answers to what a bank's vendor-risk or compliance reviewer asks before a board approves working with us — written for that review, not for a sales conversation.
This covers the BMAP Snapshot, Board Brief, and $10K Assessment — the deliverables a bank typically sees before any decision to integrate. A separate, narrower review applies once a bank moves into Full System Activation (CRM & Engagement, Retention), which is addressed in Q3.
01
What data do you receive from us?
None, at the assessment stage. BMAP's branch scoring, the Board Brief, and the $10K Assessment are built entirely from public regulatory and demographic sources — the same filings your own examiners already use. No core-banking export, customer file, or nonpublic personal information (NPI) is required to produce any of them.
FDIC Call Reports FFIEC NCUA US Census US Dept. of Labor US Housing (HUD) Zillow PostGIS geospatial
24+ public sources fused into the scoring engine — full list available on request.
What this means in practice: nothing about your account holders — balances, transactions, contact info — passes through Verlocity systems to generate a Snapshot, Board Brief, or Assessment. The analysis is about your branches and your market, built from what's already public.
02
Where does it live, and who can see it?
Public source data is ingested into a Postgres database (Supabase), and the analysis layer runs on Railway. Every client-facing view — the Command Center, the Board Brief generator — sits behind session authentication, and API access is restricted by origin (CORS) to Verlocity's own domains. All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
A shareable link to your own Command Center (?bank=your‑institution) shows only your institution's data — there is no public directory of client accounts, and internal tooling used to select institutions is gated separately from anything a client link exposes.
03
What changes if we move past the assessment into full activation?
Everything above stays true for BMAP on its own. Full System Activation — specifically the CRM & Engagement and Retention & Deepening components — is a different data relationship: it involves your own customer and prospect data to run onboarding and retention journeys. That integration does not begin under this document. It requires a separate data-processing agreement, scoped and reviewed by both sides' security and legal teams before any customer-level data moves.
