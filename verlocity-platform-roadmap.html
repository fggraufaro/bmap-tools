Now, next and later

Updated Sun 25 Oct, for the Mon 26 Oct meeting. Top 1: agree the Snapshot and Deep Dive structure and metrics. Then two goals: a first run of the new version in 30 days, and first-party bank data in 90 days so Marc’s Branch Portfolio Matrix and the custom dashboards can run.
Now · week of 26 Oct
  • Mon 26: agree the Snapshot and Deep Dive structure (sections, order, the question each answers) and the metrics behind it, with Marc’s revised mockups as the starting list.
  • Walk through Chuck’s UX/UI suggestions in Figma and pick what goes into the 30-day run.
  • Tue 27: customer review (development-partner ask; customer data only once the November checklist is met). Mockup number fixes go in before it.
  • Tue 27, 4:30 pm ET: Client kickoff with our media partner; media planning officially starts after it.
  • Teams meeting, Mickeyla and Francisco: the MediaPredict Excel tools, funnel metrics and three planning scenarios.
  • Live tools stay frozen until after the review, except security fixes.
30 days · to about 25 Nov
  • First run of the new Snapshot and Deep Dive on a real bank, with the agreed structure and metrics and Chuck’s UX changes. Public data only.
  • MediaPredict: three scenarios in every plan, modelled on our media partner’s tiers.
  • Customer-data checklist, as planned: data route tested with synthetic data (6 Nov), second sign-in step on the Hub (13 Nov), client-data environment with a two-bank separation test and access logging (20 Nov), penetration test booked, policy pack finished.
  • Start the pilot bank’s vendor review and data-sharing terms early: they set the 90-day timeline.
  • Held-back work resumes after the review: Rate Radar fixes and weekly run, Growth Plan view, company-owned accounts.
90 days · to late Jan
  • Ingest the pilot bank’s first-party data: branch-level monthly summaries first, customer-level files once the checklist and the bank’s vendor review are done.
  • Run Marc’s Branch Portfolio Matrix on that data: branch performance against market attractiveness, each branch in a quadrant with its main focus.
  • Custom dashboards from the revised mockups on the bank’s own data: branch goal plan, branch dashboard, weekly sales meeting, scorecard, team results.
  • our media partner runs audience and media planning meanwhile (a client launches in January); its brief becomes the model for the AudienceFinder and MediaPredict section of the Growth Engine.
Later · 4–12 months+
  • AudienceFinder and MediaPredict built into the Growth Engine on the our media partner structure: reach and household sizing, conversion range, three budget tiers, closed-loop measurement, day 30 and day 90 reviews.
  • Bring the elasticity tool into every customer’s dashboard: it compares the cost of winning deposits through a higher rate versus a media plan, using each branch’s real competitor rates
  • A guide for every customer: an AI assistant that reads the bank’s own data and suggests its next best move
  • First SOC 2 report (Type I) so customer data can scale beyond the pilot, then Type II; profitability measures once Finance data is in
  • Only then: plan Product B in earnest

Product A

Intelligence Subscription

Priority — build now
ForRegional banks that already have their own marketing team
StackBMAP + Rate Radar, as an ongoing subscription with a login, not a one-time report
What is leftA demo dashboard exists and works for one-off pitches. Each bank now gets its own login that shows only its own data. Still to do: switch off the old shared password, send invitation emails (waiting on the email domain setup) and add billing.
Data flowOne direction only: we send insights to the bank, and no bank data comes to us. That is why we can launch before the SOC 2 certification.
Product B

All-in-One Growth Engine

Starts after Product A proves itself
ForSmall community banks (under $1B in assets) with no marketing team of their own
StackThe full funnel: find where deposits are (BMAP), watch competitor rates (Rate Radar), pick audiences (AudienceFinder), plan the media (MediaPredict), build the web pages (Omnibranch), with a human review step
Go-to-marketWe sell direct on the East Coast, and through reseller and agency partners elsewhere
Data flowEventually banks send us their financials, which requires the SOC 2 certification
