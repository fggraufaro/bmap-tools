What happens when

Now, next and later

Now · this week · Francisco
  • Set up our email domain so banks can receive login invitations. Waiting on technical records from the web team.
  • Polish the Growth Engine demo. Walk through the four building blocks first (branch deep dive, network snapshot, competitor rates, financial brief), then the advertising step.
  • Do a full live run-through of the handoff from AudienceFinder selection to MediaPredict before showing it live.
Next · 30–90 days
  • Media Thesis meeting: shape the Activation step, from choosing an audience to running the ads.
  • Switch off the shared password once invitations work, so every customer has only their own login; then add a second sign-in step.
  • Launch customer accounts and the Product A dashboard. Logins and per-bank views work today; billing and usage tracking do not yet.
  • SOC 2 (the security certification banks ask for): an outside advisor flagged items that could stop a bank from signing. They are tracked, and we chose not to lead with them yet.
Later · 4–12 months+
  • Bring the elasticity tool into every customer’s dashboard: it already compares the cost of winning deposits through a higher rate versus a media plan, using each branch’s real competitor rates
  • A guide for every customer: an AI assistant that reads the bank’s own data and suggests its next best move
  • First SOC 2 report (Type I): banks can safely share their own data with us, which unlocks branch profitability insights
  • Auditors confirm our controls hold up over several months (Type II), and pilot banks become the proof it works
  • Only then: plan Product B in earnest

Product A

Intelligence Subscription

Priority — build now
ForRegional banks that already have their own marketing team
StackBMAP + Rate Radar, as an ongoing subscription with a login, not a one-time report
What is leftA demo dashboard exists and works for one-off pitches. Each bank now gets its own login that shows only its own data. Still to do: switch off the old shared password, send invitation emails (waiting on the email domain setup) and add billing.
Data flowOne direction only: we send insights to the bank, and no bank data comes to us. That is why we can launch before the SOC 2 certification.

Starts after Product A proves itself

StackThe full funnel: find where deposits are (BMAP), watch competitor rates (Rate Radar), pick audiences (AudienceFinder), plan the media (MediaPredict), build the web pages (Omnibranch), with a human review step
